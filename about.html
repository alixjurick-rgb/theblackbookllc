About

Lisa Jurick

Owner, The Blackbook

Lisa Jurick is the owner of The Blackbook, offering boutique home organizing, with closet editing as the signature, in Bethesda, Potomac, Chevy Chase, Rockville, and nearby Northwest DC.

Her work is focused on judgment and hands-on help deciding what to keep, not simply folding or adding storage. The approach is simple: edit what you own, and love what you keep.

Lisa comes to the home herself. Sessions are with one organizer, not a team.
