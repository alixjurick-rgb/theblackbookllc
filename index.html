The approach

Not tidying. Editing.

Folding and bins only rearrange the problem. The difference is judgment: a calm, experienced eye beside you, helping you decide with confidence what earns its place in your closet.

Every session is personal, private, and paced around you. You make every final call; we make the decisions easier and the result last.

I.

Confident purging

Guided keep, donate, and repair decisions, so the pieces you love have room to be seen and worn.

II.

Thoughtful order

Zones that match how you actually dress, so getting ready feels easy rather than effortful.

III.

Seasonal rhythm

Spring and fall changeovers that keep the closet current and the off-season stored with care.

Questions

Good to know.

What is closet editing, exactly?

It is the decision-making part of organizing. Rather than simply refolding what is there, we help you choose with confidence what to keep, donate, or repair, then organize what remains so it is easy to see and wear.

Do I need to be home during the session?

Yes, ideally. Editing works best side by side, and you approve every item that leaves your closet.

Do you sell or install organizing products?

No. Bins, dividers, shelving, installation, and hauling are not part of our packages. If something would genuinely help, we will suggest it, and any materials are approved by you first and billed separately.

How much does it cost?

Sessions start at $575, and first visits have a three-hour minimum. After a short consultation you receive a written quote for your specific project.

How often should I book?

Many clients like a Seasonal Switch each spring and fall, with a Maintenance Visit in between. We are happy to send a seasonal reminder if you would like one.
