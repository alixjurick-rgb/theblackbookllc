Questions

Good to know.

What is closet editing, exactly?

It is the decision-making part of organizing. Rather than simply refolding what is there, we help you choose with confidence what to keep, donate, or repair, then organize what remains so it is easy to see and wear.

Do you organize spaces other than closets?

Yes. Kitchens and pantries, bathrooms, home offices, kids' rooms and playrooms, and linen and utility closets. Same approach: we edit first, then organize what stays. Each project gets a written quote after a free consultation.

Do I need to be home during the session?

Yes, ideally. Editing works best side by side, and you approve every item that leaves the space.

Is the work confidential?

Yes. Sessions are private, and your details stay private. We do not sell or share personal information, and we never photograph or share your home or the spaces we edit without your separate, explicit permission.

Do you sell or install organizing products?

No. Organizing products, shelving, installation, hauling, and disposal are not part of our packages. If something would genuinely help, we will suggest it, and any materials are approved by you first and billed separately.

How much does it cost?

The Closet Edit is three hours for $575. The Seasonal Switch is four hours for $750. The Complete Reset is six hours for $1,125. A Maintenance Visit is two hours for $375, for returning clients. A first visit has a three-hour minimum. Larger or more complex projects receive a custom written quote after a short consultation.

How long does a typical session take?

Most first visits are three, four, or six hours, depending on the package. A first visit has a three-hour minimum.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. It is a complimentary 15-minute call to learn about the space and prepare a written quote.

How do I prepare?

No need to tidy first. Be home for the session. Photos are helpful and can be sent by email after you request a consultation.

What happens to donations?

You approve every item that leaves. You decide what is donated or kept. We do not name a specific charity.

What if I get emotional or feel indecisive?

The session is paced around you. We guide; you make every final call.

Do you work with couples or shared closets?

Yes. Shared closets are welcome. It helps when everyone who shares the space can take part in the decisions.

Who comes to my home?

Lisa Jurick, the owner. Sessions are with one organizer, not a team.

What if we need more time?

Any additional time is $195 per hour, only with your approval first.

How often should I book?

Many clients like a Seasonal Switch each spring and fall, with a Maintenance Visit in between. We are happy to send a seasonal reminder if you would like one.
